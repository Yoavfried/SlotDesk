28 October 2026.

## Improved

- Trip entry uses departure and destination cities, with the departure city prefilled from the profile when identifiable.
- Arrival times and timezones are calculated from the selected route. Matched calendar flights supply recorded times; other flights show a geographic estimate.
- Connection flight adds a connection city and its departure date/time while keeping the final destination visible. Return flights prefill the trip origin.
- City search keeps separate coordinates for cities sharing a timezone, including San Francisco and Los Angeles.

## Fixed

- Removed required arrival date, arrival time and timezone fields from the trip editor.
- Preserved calculated arrival instants across date changes and daylight-saving clock changes.

The Chromium ZIP is the desktop extension package. The Firefox XPI is unsigned; the Safari archive contains source only.

To update an unpacked installation, replace the files in its existing folder, reload the extension and reopen SlotDesk. Keep the existing installation to retain saved profiles and settings.
