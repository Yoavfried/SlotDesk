## 0.6.0 ranking and evidence refinement

After worst and total required-person inconvenient minutes, compare worst and total distance from applicable work intervals (or convenience intervals when working rules are ignored), then comfortable margin, optional attendance and start time. Positive availability remains authoritative and protected time is not relaxed. Boundary starts supplement the configured grid and respect blocked/inconvenient radii. Alternatives have actual results and valid duration/date changes; the original fixed-heading presentation is superseded.

Accepted/tentative accessible invitations provide only that person's known commitments. A No removes that invitation's commitment; unknown/unanswered responses do not establish a commitment. Full availability still requires complete fresh coverage of mapped sources. Guest-response display does not require the guest's private-calendar access.
