## Why matching remains visible

Primary calendar IDs usually match the primary email. CalendarList also supplies secondary-calendar dataOwner. The primary flag means the authenticated account's primary calendar, while accessRole describes permission. Neither owning a team calendar nor appearing on one appointment proves that every event occupies that person's time. Use clear identity matches and confirmed source choices, rather than names/domains/permissions as ownership guesses. [Google calendar concepts](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/concepts/events-calendars), [CalendarList fields](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/v3/reference/calendarList).

Reading an invitation's guest responses requires access to that event, not the guest's private calendar. Availability mappings, the organizing calendar and the Existing meetings selection serve different purposes.

## Future feature: declined-invitation follow-up

**Implemented in 0.7.0 after authorization on 13 October.** The bell groups upcoming No responses, distinguishes optional guests and offers Find another time/Dismiss. Normal calendar reads and Refresh update observations; repeated dismissed No replies stay quiet. No response automatically moves, cancels, replaces or resends anything. See [the accepted flow](rescheduling.md).
