“A good time for everyone,” “A good time starts here,” and “Less back and forth. More room.” are contextual interface copy. They do not replace the main brand line. Speedy's “Tools for the work that eats your week” describes the wider team and its tools.

## Interface terms

- Use sentence case for ordinary headings, buttons and labels. Preserve brand names and the uppercase wordmark and eyebrow text.
- Main navigation is **New meeting**, **Existing meetings** and **Manage profiles**.
- **Travel & flights** opens the **Trips** manager. Its actions include **Add trip**, **Add from calendar**, **Connection flight** and **Flight back**.
- Use **timezone** as one word in prose. Distinguish a person's base timezone from timezone changes during a trip.
- Use **meeting** for the scheduled item. An **invitation** is sent to its participants. Use **calendar** for a calendar source and **profile** for saved information and scheduling preferences about a person.
- **Generate planning email** creates editable text to copy. It does not send an email or read replies.

## Capability claims

- The current beta supports Google Calendar. Do not advertise Outlook, Safari installation, mobile support or cross-device settings sync as available.
- During the closed beta, Google connection requires an approved test account. Draft uploads and unsigned packages are not published store installations.
- Users review and confirm new invitations, meeting edits and rescheduling. SlotDesk never deletes appointments or automatically reschedules other meetings. Removing a guest can withdraw their invitation.
- Profiles and settings are browser-local. Avoid blanket claims that all data stays on the device: Google processes calendar requests, the sign-in service processes authorization, and onboarding can send the registration record described in the privacy notice.
- Flight arrivals can be estimated from the route or taken from a linked calendar event. Do not promise live flight tracking or exact airline schedules.
- Say **Available** only when known calendar and scheduling rules support it; missing calendar coverage is not confirmed free time.
- Do not publish a fixed automated-test count as a product promise. Describe automated checks without implying they certify live Google access or store approval.
- Add store badges and download links only when the matching installation destination exists. Identify beta access accurately until public distribution is available.
