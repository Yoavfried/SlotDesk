01 / Install or update

  1. Extract the entire ZIP into a permanent folder.
  2. Open your browser’s Extensions manager: chrome://extensions, edge://extensions or vivaldi://extensions. Enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and select the folder containing manifest.json.
  3. Already installed? Replace the contents of its installed folder with this build, then click Reload on its extension card. Keep the same installed folder to retain its saved profiles.
  4. Open SlotDesk from your browser’s Extensions menu. A full workspace tab opens when a native sidebar is unavailable. Use the gear to open Settings, then Open full workspace for a larger tab.

02 / Make it yours

  1. Click Connect Google Calendar and grant the requested calendar access. This build can create events and send invitations after your review.
  2. Select the calendars SlotDesk can use. Events in excluded calendars will not appear or affect suggestions. Change this selection later in Settings.
  3. Confirm your own email, name and timezone, and link selected calendars that describe your availability. A name may be suggested from your calendar entries; you can edit it. Other preferences are inside More settings.
  4. Optionally add familiar people, or skip this step. Finish all three steps to complete onboarding. If setup is interrupted, it starts again at calendar selection with saved entries available to prefill. Completed users keep their workspace.

Access blocked? Ask the app owner to check the test-user list and requested scopes. You do not create a Google Cloud project.

03 / Find, review, send

  1. Add guests by saved profile or email. Everyone mapped to the organizing calendar joins by default; you can remove anyone. Confirm unsaved guests’ timezones before searching. Confirm each person’s timezone before searching.
  2. Choose dates and duration, then click Find a time.
  3. Choose an option and review the title, calendar, participants, local times, Meet link option and warnings.
  4. Use Create & send invitations, or Copy meeting details to create it manually. Everyone retained in the participant list is invited.

Sending from this workspace creates a real Google Calendar meeting and notifies the participants shown in your review.
