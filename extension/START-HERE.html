01 / Install or update

  1. Extract the entire ZIP into a permanent folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome. Enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and select the folder containing manifest.json.
  3. Already installed? Replace the contents of its installed folder with this build, then click Reload on its extension card. Keep the same installed folder to retain its saved profiles.
  4. Open SlotDesk from Chrome’s Extensions menu. Use the gear to open Settings, then Open full workspace for a larger tab.

02 / Make it yours

  1. Click Connect Google and grant the requested Calendar access. This build can create events and send invitations after your review.
  2. Confirm your own email, name and timezone, and link calendars that describe your availability. Other preferences are inside Advanced.
  3. Select the calendars SlotDesk can use and your default invitation calendar. Events in excluded calendars will not appear or affect suggestions. Change this selection later in Settings.
  4. Optionally add familiar people, or skip this step. Existing users keep their profiles and only need to confirm calendar selection.

Access blocked? Ask the app owner to check the test-user list and requested scopes. You do not create a Google Cloud project.

03 / Find, review, send

  1. Add guests by saved profile or email. Everyone mapped to the organizing calendar joins by default; you can remove anyone. Confirm unsaved guests’ timezones before searching. A new email opens a short profile setup. Confirm each person’s timezone.
  2. Choose dates and duration, then click Find a time.
  3. Choose an option and review the title, calendar, participants, local times, Meet link option and warnings.
  4. Use Create & send invitations, or Copy meeting details to create it manually. Everyone retained in the participant list is invited.

Sending from this workspace creates a real Google Calendar meeting and notifies the participants shown in your review.
