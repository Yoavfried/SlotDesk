Getting started

Confirm your own profile, choose the calendars SlotDesk should use and a default invitation calendar when a writable source is selected, then optionally add familiar people. You can skip the people step and return to Manage profiles later. The final step explains the five-field registration record and offers an optional, unchecked project-updates choice.

Events in calendars you leave out do not appear or affect suggestions. Change the selection under Settings → Calendars. The calendar picker in Existing meetings only changes what you see within that selection.

Profiles and guests

Availability calendars are in the main profile form. Choose the no-access option when needed; availability stays unconfirmed. Use Travel & flights in Manage profiles to edit travel directly. Timezone dropdowns search cities, capitals, countries, common timezone names and spelling variants. For example, San Francisco finds America/Los_Angeles; Eastern finds US Eastern choices. Countries with several timezones show several choices. Choose a result to confirm the timezone; typing alone does not change it.

You can add a guest by email without saving a profile. Confirm their timezone for the meeting before searching, or use Create profile. Everyone mapped to the organizing calendar is added automatically; you can remove them, and manual additions stay when you switch calendars.

Flexible scheduling

Advanced profile options hold separate working-day/hour, usual-convenience and ordinary-overlap preferences. Options for this meeting or search can override them temporarily. Ignoring usual convenience does not ignore flight buffers, other inconvenient periods or protected time. Allowed overlaps remain visible and never move other appointments automatically. Automatically detected calendar time off and title-based protection guesses on your own profile can appear as warned overlap alternatives. Explicit protections, saved flights and other people’s time off still block proposals. When protection prevents all options, the results identify the blocking entries so you can review their profile rules.

Find a time

Connect Google, save your own profile and choose a default invitation calendar. Set up familiar faces if you wish, then continue to the workspace menu and choose New meeting. Search saved profiles by name or email and add the people attending. A new email can be added immediately; confirm its meeting timezone before searching, or choose Create profile to save the person. Confirm each person’s timezone, choose dates and duration, then select Find a time. Automatic uses 10-minute starts, or 5-minute starts when they avoid conflicts or improve full optional attendance without worsening required fit. Suggested starts never use units smaller than five minutes. Advanced retains Start-time spacing and Options per day: leave them Automatic or choose a custom grid/maximum. Custom spacing uses multiples of five.

You take part only when your profile is included. Use Manage profiles to edit saved people. New meeting clears the title and attendee selections while keeping your saved profiles.

How meeting options are chosen

Explicit flight buffers and marked inconvenient periods take priority over ordinary working-hour preferences. Required guests are treated equally. If you allow your own overlaps, guest working hours can rank ahead of a clash-free time outside their working hours. When guests fit equally well, a clash-free time takes priority over your ordinary office hours. Explicit protections and saved flights still apply.

Options keep your requested duration and dates first, then clearly labelled shorter or later alternatives. Equally good suggestions use the earliest date and vary morning, midday, afternoon or evening before moving dates. The list shows the actual selected count. Each person’s fit distinguishes meeting overlaps, day off, working hours, flight buffers and unconfirmed availability; expand Warnings for the actual entries and times. Existing appointments stay in place unless you separately review and confirm a change.

Existing meetings

From the workspace menu, select Existing meetings. Choose start and end dates, select one or more calendars, and adjust Times shown in if needed. SlotDesk loads all accessible calendars for those dates; adding or removing a calendar updates the list from memory. Changing the dates or selecting Refresh meetings loads the range again. View details shows guest responses, optional status and Google Calendar/Meet links. Created with SlotDesk identifies events whose SlotDesk metadata is available. Use Refresh meetings after a guest responds to load the latest response. Busy-only access cannot show event details; incomplete reads may omit meetings. The red Overlapping label names conflicts in the displayed calendars through expandable details. Shared copies do not conflict with themselves. Reschedule meeting opens a separate review before any time changes.

Refresh meetings is beside the meeting count. If loading was stopped, a notice above it offers Refresh to finish loading. Returning after leaving during a load starts it again automatically. Completed loads stay cached.

Edit a meeting

Open View details → Edit meeting to change the title, location, description or guest list. Search saved profiles or enter an email and confirm the new person’s timezone. Add/remove guests and set Required or Optional, then review the exact changes. Removed guests are no longer invited; Google may remove their invitation from their calendars. The organizing meeting stays.

Save changes & notify guests asks Google to apply the changes and notify guests. Dates, times, the existing Meet link and other untouched details are kept. Use Reschedule meeting for time changes. Recurring edits affect only this occurrence. A complete supported guest list and organizer write access are required for guest changes; other details can still be edited when the roster is incomplete.

If confirmation is lost, the bell shows a pending alert. SlotDesk checks the same meeting automatically, including after reopening. A verified failure offers Try again with the saved review. Refresh details if someone else changed the meeting.

Reschedule a meeting

Open a meeting’s details and select Reschedule meeting. Search for another time or enter exact dates/times. Confirm missing guest timezones through short profile setup. Review the current and new times, guests and warnings, then select Reschedule & notify guests. The meeting details and video link are kept; a recurring change affects only this occurrence. All-day appointments keep their date-only format.

Only the organizing calendar with write access can apply a change. Other appointments are never moved automatically. A conflict proposal offers replacement times for the other meetings; confirmed conflicts remain in Appointments to reschedule in Existing meetings until handled or dismissed. A bell alert offers Reschedule old appointment and Reschedule SlotDesk appointment. Each opens a separate review; if several appointments are involved, choose one. The main menu stays clear.

If confirmation is lost, SlotDesk adds a bell alert and checks the same appointment automatically. A verified failure offers Try again. If someone else changed it during review, refresh its details instead of overwriting their edit.

Meeting alerts

The bell shows upcoming invitations where a guest answered no, with optional guests identified. Find another time opens rescheduling. Dismiss keeps the same observed response quiet. Responses update during normal calendar reads and Refresh meetings; these response alerts update while you work. The bell also shows unconfirmed calendar changes and their automatically checked results. Checks never send changes automatically.

Profiles

Manage profiles has searchable saved people, Add profile and A few familiar faces? suggestions. Confirm a suggested person’s timezone before saving. Profiles are separate from the participant list of a new meeting.

Settings and appearance

Use the gear beside the logo to open Settings. Dark mode and Open full workspace are at the top. You can also change time/date display and the default invitation calendar. Changes save immediately. Choose calendars opens the source selector with Save and Discard. Clear data is at the bottom. Profile editing is in Manage profiles.

Connection and loading recovery

Reconnect appears beside your account when Google needs sign-in again. Reconnection keeps this account’s saved workspace and does not send an invitation. Stop loading cancels reads, not an already sent calendar change. Temporary refresh failures can keep an older in-memory meeting list, labelled Outdated with its last check. Unknown or stale availability is never confirmed free.

Read-only selected calendars support browsing and availability searches. Select a writable calendar in Settings → Calendars before creating invitations. New calendars are offered for selection and are not included automatically.

If saved browser data cannot be read, SlotDesk preserves it and offers Try loading again and Download saved data. Reset is an explicit local action after checking for unfinished calendar changes. Removing browser data or using another device does not recover local profiles or unfinished operations.

Google connection

Connection details shows this browser’s capabilities and safe information you can copy for support.

The email badge below the logo shows the connected Google account. The exit icon beside it disconnects that account. The independent connection opens Google’s account choice and permission window. Allow the requested calendar event, calendar list and free/busy access. Your browser account or Sync is not required for this path.

Sign-in fails before Google opens

When the new connection service is inactive, the Chrome pilot can still use its browser-managed Google connection. Being signed in to Gmail on a website does not guarantee that legacy service is available. G101 means the required sign-in method is unavailable; G102 means browser-level Google sign-in is disabled or unavailable. Open SlotDesk in a Chrome profile with Google sign-in enabled. This build can use a separate Google sign-in window once the app owner has activated the browser-independent connection. G110 means that setup is pending; G111 means this package’s return address needs registration.

Other sign-in messages

G103: reconnect Google; the previous connection expired or is unavailable. G104: ask your organization’s administrator to review the explicit access block. G105: ask the person who provided SlotDesk to check its Google configuration. G106: reconnect and allow calendar events, calendar list and availability access. G107: check your connection or wait briefly if sign-in is temporarily limited. G108: the browser returned no sign-in token; try connecting again. G109: the sign-in window was closed or permission was declined. G112: reload the extension because its background connection is unavailable. G114: reconnect because the sign-in result could not be verified. G199: the failure could not be identified; report the reference and which step failed. Never send access tokens or calendar exports for a sign-in report.

Access is blocked

Ask the app owner to add your Google email to the private pilot’s test audience and check the requested scopes. Your Workspace administrator may also need to allow SlotDesk. You do not create a Google Cloud project.

A calendar is missing

Open Google Calendar with the connected account and check sharing and the calendar list. Reconnect or search again to refresh available calendars. Only selected, writable calendars are offered as organizing calendars. Also check Settings → Calendars if a source is missing.

Calendar status and participant fit

Each participant’s calendar icon indicates whether their selected sources cover this time. Missing or incomplete sources leave availability unconfirmed, including for optional guests. This is separate from whether a time fits or has conflicts, and it does not mean guests have accepted invitations.

Each participant row explains whether the time fits their calendar and profile rules. Availability unconfirmed means calendar data is missing or incomplete. A known conflict is shown even when other data is incomplete. Local times appear when their timezone differs from the proposal.

Availability is unconfirmed

Use the calendar icon beside a participant to check coverage. Edit the person’s profile → Availability calendars to check sources. A saved profile or stated availability does not give access to that person’s calendar.

Review and send

Review the title, organizing calendar, participants, full local dates/times and warnings. Everyone retained in the participant list is invited. Copy meeting details if you want to create the event manually. Create & send invitations creates a real new event and notifies its participants.

A send could not be confirmed

The meeting window keeps your details. A bell alert says the result could not be confirmed. SlotDesk checks the saved appointment automatically at spaced intervals while open, pauses offline and resumes when connected or reopened. Success updates the alert without another invitation. After repeated checks establish that the change did not go through, Try again opens the saved review. Discard change rechecks the result before discarding the unsaved attempt; it does not delete an appointment. A retry needs your confirmation and uses the same appointment identity. If Google shows a different change or access is unavailable, SlotDesk keeps the operation for review instead of resending it.

When there is no connection, creating, editing and rescheduling meetings are blocked. Saved profiles and drafts remain available. Exceptional manual resolution remains available when an operation cannot be verified, with an acknowledgement and a retained operation record.

When creation succeeds, Meeting created appears over the workspace menu. Use Done to continue or View meeting to open its calendar list.

Google Meet is still pending

An event can be created successfully while Google prepares the video link. Open the created event or use Check video link. If Google reports a conference failure, add a link in Google Calendar.

Profiles, flights and portable files

Valid profile changes save automatically on this device. Invalid settings and saving problems show an error and keep the last saved value. Invalid email edits restore the saved address with an alert. Your first own-profile setup uses Continue.

Travel & flights opens the person’s saved entries. Add flight opens a form with Save and Discard. Return flight fills reversed locations and timezones; enter both return dates and times. Save keeps both as separate flights. Each can later be edited or removed independently. There is no saved flight form to resume: Save or Discard is required to exit. Whole-day travel uses the same per-entry actions. These rules never change calendar appointments.

Settings → Download backup saves a portable copy of profiles/settings. Restore backup replaces the local setup and clears an unfinished meeting. My profile changes only from a matching-email record; otherwise it stays. Onboarding still requires your own profile. Download current backup is optional before replacement. Calendar events and Google sign-in stay unchanged.

Manage profiles → Export profiles or Import profiles lets you select people, including Select all. Imports replace matching-email people after one warning, leave others alone and cannot change your own profile. Missing optional columns use defaults with a warning; missing/invalid email or timezone stops the import. Calendar links are included, but cannot grant Google access. Unavailable/excluded sources remain unconfirmed. If access could not be checked, use Check calendar access in Settings before sending.

Resolve an unconfirmed calendar change before importing/restoring. Files contain personal profile and travel information in plain JSON; store and share them carefully. They exclude Google credentials, loaded appointments, registration records and meeting drafts.

Keyboard navigation

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls. The Skip to workspace link bypasses the header. Enter or Space activates a focused button. In profile suggestions and timezone search, use the arrow keys to choose and Enter to select; Escape closes the choices first.

Popup headings receive focus so you can identify the screen. Closing a child popup returns you to its action in the parent. Flight and travel forms require Save or Discard; Escape never discards their values. Enter while typing in meeting review does not send invitations. Focus Create & send invitations when you intend to send.

Invalid fields have associated messages; relevant advanced sections open to show the problem. Dark mode is in Settings, and browser zoom and system contrast preferences remain available.

Privacy and saved data

Saved profiles, preferences and unfinished invitation details stay in this browser. Loaded calendar data is cleared from connected SlotDesk windows when you disconnect. Invitations are sent after your review and confirmation.

Before using a work account

Confirm that your organization permits the extension, requested Google access and local data handling before connecting a work account. This build creates new meetings and supports reviewed individual time and guest/detail changes. It does not delete appointments or move them between calendars. Google’s event permission is broader than that application behavior.
